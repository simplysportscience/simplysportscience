Essential Amino Acids

Amino acids the body cannot make, so they must come from food.

📖What it means

Essential amino acids are amino acids that the body cannot synthesise itself, so they must be obtained from the diet. There are nine essential amino acids for adults, including leucine, which is particularly important for stimulating muscle protein synthesis.

Athletes need adequate intake of essential amino acids for muscle repair and adaptation. Complete protein sources, such as meat, fish, eggs and dairy, contain all nine, while some plant sources may need combining to provide them all.

🏅Sporting examples

Recovery

Leucine in a protein-rich meal stimulates muscle protein synthesis after training.

Diet

A vegan athlete combines plant proteins to get all the essential amino acids.

Muscle

Essential amino acids are needed to build new muscle protein.

✍️Using the term in context

Explain why a vegan athlete should combine protein sources:

"Essential amino acids cannot be made by the body, so a vegan athlete should combine plant protein sources to make sure they receive all nine essential amino acids for muscle repair."
