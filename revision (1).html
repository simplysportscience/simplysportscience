A simple way to revise

Choose a focus

Pick a course, a theme or a topic. Start with the topic you find hardest.

Answer first

Write or say your answer before looking at the model response. This is the step that builds memory.

Check the points

Tick the key points you included, then see how many you covered.

Come back later

Revisit weak topics after a few days. Spacing beats cramming every time.

Study habits that work
Test yourself, do not just re-readRecall is stronger than re-reading. Close the page and write what you remember.
Learn the command wordsKnow what "evaluate" or "explain" requires before you write. Each course has its own guide.
Use real sporting examplesExaminers reward application. For each idea, name a sport and a performer.
Watch first, then readUse the video or presentation for the big picture, then the topic page for detail.
Plan short sessionsRecall for 5 minutes, study for 20, practise for 10, then review what was hardest.
Check the mark pointsCompare your answer with the points. Missing points show you what to revisit.
