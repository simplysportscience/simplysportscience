The Story

Why I Built Simply Sport Science

My journey into teaching wasn't a straight line. I started my career in sales — but it didn't take long to realise that what I really wanted to do was work in physical education. A conversation with my wife was the turning point. I retrained, qualified as a PE teacher, and never looked back. Even during my time in sales, I stayed connected to sport through youth sports coaching.

Over 20 years of teaching PE across UK and international schools, I have worked with students and teachers across a wide range of courses, from IB Sports, Exercise and Health Science through to BTEC Sport. Teaching IB SEHS is where the need for Simply Sport Science became most apparent. The syllabus is vast and genuinely demanding — and the freely available resources online were nowhere near good enough.

"I built the resource I wished I had when I first started teaching sport science — something that explains the content clearly, uses the right specification language, and helps students apply it in their exam."

Simply Sport Science started as videos and resources shared with my own students. When teachers from other schools asked for the same materials, I realised there was a genuine need for a proper platform — specification-accurate, professionally presented and useful for both students and teachers.

My research interest in retrieval practice — developed during my PGCE Top-up at the University of Northampton and continuing into my PhD — has shaped how this platform is designed. Not just what to teach, but how to make it stick. Alongside the platform, my books Happiness Factories and Changemakers explore the broader philosophy of holistic, movement-centred physical education.
