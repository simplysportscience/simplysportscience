Revision Tips

What actually works

  • Test yourself, don't just re-read. Use practice checks after every topic, not just before exams.
  • Space it out. Study a topic, leave it a few days, then revisit — this beats cramming every time.
  • Learn the command words. Know exactly what "evaluate" or "explain" requires before you write.
  • Use real sporting examples. Examiners reward application, not just definitions.
  • Watch, then read. Video first for the big picture, then the lesson page for detail.
