Ethical Issues and Performance Aids in Sport

Sport depends on trust: that competitors play by the rules, that officials are fair and that athletes are not harmed. In this unit you will explore ethical values in sport, the issues that challenge them, the use of performance aids, and the role of organisations in upholding ethical standards.

0

Overview

Sport depends on trust: that competitors play by the rules, that officials are fair and that athletes are not harmed. In this unit you will explore ethical values in sport, the issues that challenge them, the use of performance aids, and the role of organisations in upholding ethical standards.

1

A1 Ethical values in sport

Core values include fair play, integrity, respect, responsibility, and care for mental health and the environment. Each value can be tested by a real situation. For example, fair play is tested when a player is tempted to take advantage of a referee's mistake.

2

A2 Emerging ethical issues

Current issues include commercial sponsorship that conflicts with health, athlete activism, the use of technology in officiating, online abuse of athletes, sustainability and equality. Consider how each issue affects athletes, fans and organisations.

3

B1 Types of performance aids

Performance aids include legal aids such as sports drinks and some supplements, and substances or methods that are prohibited. Each is intended to improve performance, and each has different levels of regulation and risk.

4

B2 Impact of performance aids on fairness and health

Judge each aid by three questions: what is the intended benefit, what is the health risk, and is it regulated? An aid that gives an unfair advantage or harms health is an ethical problem, even if it is not against the rules in every setting.

5

B3 Regulation and monitoring

Governing bodies publish lists of prohibited substances and methods, test athletes and apply sanctions. Testing protects fairness and health. Explain the role of monitoring and the limits of it, such as the cost of testing.

6

C1 Key organisations

Governing bodies, clubs, schools and event organisers all have a role in promoting ethical values. Each sets codes of conduct and responds to breaches.

7

C2 Organisational strategies

Organisations use codes of conduct, education, monitoring and sanctions. Evaluate each strategy by whether it changes behaviour, not just whether it exists.

8

C3 Challenges

Organisations face challenges such as limited budgets, global competition, online behaviour that is hard to police and pressure to win. A strategy must be realistic for the organisation to succeed.

9

D1 Strategies for individual athletes

Individuals can uphold ethical values through honest behaviour, respect for opponents and officials, asking for help when under pressure, and reporting concerns.

10

D2 Organisational strategies

Organisations can run education programmes, clear reporting routes, fair selection, and visible sanctions. Recommend the strategy that fits the setting and explain how its impact would be measured.

11

D3 Event-level strategies

Events can use codes of conduct for spectators, stewarding, anti-abuse policies and clear rules for officials. These protect participants and the public.

12

Practice

Write or say your answer before you open the points. Use the points to check what you covered, not to copy.

3 marksPractice 1

Explain key ethical issues in sport.

Your answer should include:
  • Name one issue, such as sponsorship or online abuse.
  • Explain how it affects athletes or organisations.
  • Link it to a value, such as fair play or respect.
6 marksPractice 2

Analyse the advantages and disadvantages of performance aids for athletes and organisations.

Your answer should include:
  • Give an advantage and a disadvantage for athletes.
  • Explain the role of regulation.
  • Give an advantage and a disadvantage for organisations, and make a judgement.
13

Key terms

IntegrityHonesty and consistency in performance and in decisions, free from corruption.
GamesmanshipUsing legal but dishonourable tactics to gain an advantage.
Code of conductA set of standards that describes the behaviour expected of members of an organisation.
Athlete activismThe use of sport as a platform to raise social or political issues.

Check your understanding

Tick each statement you can do confidently. Any you cannot tick are the parts to revisit.

  • I can evaluate a performance aid by its benefit, health risk and regulation.
  • I can describe how an organisation promotes ethical behaviour.
  • I can recommend a feasible strategy and say how to measure it.
