Component 2 Coursework · 35 marks AS Level 8386

Goalball

The skills, tactical awareness, filming and log requirements for goalball in AS Level coursework.

1

Assessment requirements

Assessed in the selection and application of skills and tactical awareness in competitive conditioned practices and formal competitive games. All players must wear eyeshades so that they use their other senses, not sight.

Log of competitive participationLog every competitive match. Do not include training sessions in the log. The log must be completed for every candidate in the centre, and it supports the mark for each strand.
2

Skills

Skills

  • Orientation and mobility: in attack and defence.
  • Anticipation: spatial awareness of court and team positions, auditory localisation, reaction time, awareness of timekeeping.
  • Defence: from the ready position to the barrier position.
  • Attack: transfer to the feet and lunge to release the ball.
  • Throwing: straight smooth shot, bounce shot, rotation shot, with the ball touching the court in each required zone, using the dominant and non-dominant hand.
3

Tactical awareness

  • Selection of appropriate individual attacking and defensive movements during competitive situations.
  • Awareness of team tactics and strategies in attacking and defensive situations, including the application of different systems in different situations.
  • Awareness of the strengths and weaknesses of self and other players, and use of strategies to enhance the chance of success.
  • Awareness and application of the rules and regulations of the sport.
4

Filming and evidence

Show the candidate’s throws into each zone, and their defensive positioning.

Whatever the activity, the moderator must be able to identify the candidate. Use coloured bibs with contrasting numbers on the front and back, or the method the centre uses on its Centre Order of Merit form. Evidence may be edited to focus on a candidate and to remove periods of irrelevance, but it must not be edited to make the candidate look more consistent or more able than they normally are. The speed settings of filmed evidence must not be adjusted.

5

Marking focus

Each activity is marked using the generic assessment criteria, with the specific guidance above. The four strands are marked as follows:

  • Range of skills (5): how many of the skills above are performed in authentic competitive situations.
  • Quality of skills (10): control, fluency and accuracy in the skills performed.
  • Tactical awareness (10): selecting the most appropriate skills, tactics and strategies, and understanding the performance of others.
  • Effective performance (10): maintaining the range and quality of skills under pressure, and the influence the candidate has on the outcome. This is supported by the log of competitive participation.
6

Self-check

Use this list before a filming session or before submitting evidence.

  • Orientation and mobility: in attack and defence.
  • Anticipation: spatial awareness of court and team positions, auditory localisation, reaction time, awareness of timekeeping.
  • I have a complete log of every competitive participation, with the details the syllabus requires.
